# CallGuard AI Personas

All personas and organizations described here are fictional.

## Carrier network product lead

Owns call protection for millions of subscribers. Success means fewer spam and
fraud complaints without blocking schools, healthcare providers, banks, or other
legitimate callers. Needs low latency at very high volume, safe model rollouts,
carrier-specific controls, and clear incident communication.

## Bank fraud and authentication leader

Protects customers from impersonation and social-engineering calls. Values fraud
recall, explainable risk signals, audit trails, and dependable delivery. Is highly
sensitive to model blind spots but also to false positives affecting legitimate
customer-service and authentication calls.

## Contact-center operations director

Needs outbound calls to reach customers and inbound calls to be routed correctly.
Tracks answer rates, agent productivity, call labeling, and customer complaints.
Wants configurable policies and fast support more than sophisticated ML language.

## Communications-platform product manager

Embeds CallGuard AI into another developer product. Prioritizes API stability,
documentation, multi-tenant controls, predictable pricing, and webhook reliability.
Needs primitives that serve many downstream use cases rather than a rigid workflow.

## Trust and safety analyst

Investigates suspicious traffic and tunes policies. Uses reason codes and the
explainability experience frequently, but only derives value when the information
changes a decision, speeds an investigation, or prevents an incident.

## Integration engineer / SRE

Implements and operates the API. Cares about p95/p99 latency, uptime, rate limits,
retry behavior, observability, versioning, and incident response. Needs changes to
be backward compatible and regional performance to be visible.
