# CallGuard AI Roadmap and Recent Releases

This fictional roadmap supplies context for analysis; it is not a commitment.

## Recent releases

### January 2025 — Model v3.0

Established the baseline ensemble for spam, fraud, and robocall classification.

### May 2025 — Model v3.1

Improved calibration and carrier reputation features, with a focus on balanced
precision and recall.

### July 2025 — EU routing modernization

Changed EU inference routing and cache topology to prepare for growth. Regional
latency guardrails and rollback readiness are part of the follow-up review.

### September 2025 — Model v3.2

Added graph-based features and tuned thresholds toward higher unwanted-call recall.
The rollout requires close monitoring of false positives and segment-level customer
complaints.

## Current roadmap

### Now

- Diagnose v3.2 quality outcomes by carrier and customer segment.
- Restore EU latency to the service-level objective.
- Protect renewals for high-value accounts showing quality or reliability risk.

### Next

- Add per-customer threshold simulation before policy changes.
- Improve legitimate-business allowlisting and appeal workflows.
- Redesign explainability around actions and investigation outcomes, not page views.

### Later

- Expand multi-region failover and capacity automation.
- Add customer-controlled evaluation sets and release scorecards.
- Explore new channels only after call reputation quality and reliability stabilize.
