# CallGuard AI Company Overview

> **Synthetic company:** CallGuard AI is entirely fictional. All names, customers,
> metrics, incidents, and commercial details in this repository are invented for
> product-management learning and evaluation.

## Mission

CallGuard AI helps people and businesses trust incoming calls. Its mission is to
reduce harmful and unwanted calling without preventing legitimate communication.

## Product and business model

CallGuard AI sells a real-time call reputation API to mobile carriers, banks,
contact centers, and communications platforms. For each call, the API returns a
classification—`legitimate`, `spam`, `fraud`, or `robocall`—plus a confidence score.
Customers use the result to label, route, challenge, or block calls.

The company operates a usage-based B2B SaaS model with annual contracts. Pricing
combines a platform fee, API-volume tiers, and enterprise support. Large carriers
and communications platforms generate the most volume, while regulated banks tend
to place the highest value on fraud detection, auditability, and reliability.

## Company-level goals

- Improve protection from spam, fraud, and robocalls.
- Minimize false positives that interrupt legitimate calls.
- Meet enterprise latency and uptime commitments across regions.
- Grow API volume and retention without trading away customer trust.
- Give customer teams enough explanation and control to act confidently.

## Core metrics

The product team monitors unwanted-call precision and recall, legitimate-call false
positive rate, p95 latency, uptime, API volume, support complaint rate, expansion
ARR, gross retention, and retention risk. Metrics must be segmented by customer,
customer segment, region, carrier, and model version because aggregate averages can
hide concentrated harm.
