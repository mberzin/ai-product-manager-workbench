# CallGuard AI Product Strategy

## Strategic objective

Become the most trusted real-time decision layer for call reputation by balancing
protection, legitimate-call safety, and enterprise-grade reliability.

## Product principles

1. **Optimize for trust, not a single model metric.** Recall gains are valuable only
   when precision, false-positive complaints, and legitimate-call outcomes remain
   acceptable.
2. **Segment before deciding.** Model and workflow performance must be evaluated by
   carrier, customer segment, geography, and use case—not only in aggregate.
3. **Reliability is product quality.** Latency and uptime directly affect customer
   workflows and renewal decisions.
4. **Measure outcomes beyond adoption.** Feature usage is a leading signal, not proof
   of customer or business value.
5. **Roll out reversibly.** Model and infrastructure changes require canaries,
   guardrails, customer-level monitoring, and rollback plans.

## Current bets

- Improve fraud and robocall recall using graph and reputation features.
- Reduce legitimate-call false positives with better calibration and allowlists.
- Build regional routing and capacity management for predictable latency.
- Give enterprise customers configurable thresholds and policy controls.
- Connect explanation views to analyst actions and measurable investigation value.

## Explicit non-goals for this learning phase

The repository does not represent a real company strategy. It does not contain real
customers, employer information, production models, or production performance.
